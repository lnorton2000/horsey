Rankings

Rating = recency- and confidence-weighted average of each horse's race-level unified figures. Spread = how far the products disagree about the same run.

Calibration

Unified = intercept + slope × figure × (8 ÷ furlongs, for length-based sheets only). Products run on different scales (ThoroManager and Equibase, for example, run about 10 points above Beyer), so each one is converted before figures are blended or compared. With automatic calibration, each product with at least 20 races that also have another product's figure is fitted from your saved races against the consensus of the others (Beyer is the fixed anchor), and refitted as you add races.
