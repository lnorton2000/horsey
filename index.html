Figures

One row per race run. Sheet notation like 12-, 9", F7+ is understood. Distances like 6f, 1 1/16m, 1m70y.

Rankings

Rating = recency- and confidence-weighted average of each horse's race-level unified figures. Spread = how far the products disagree about the same run.

Calibration

Unified = intercept + slope × figure × (8 ÷ furlongs, for length-based sheets only). Defaults are rules of thumb. With a few dozen races where you hold two or more products, Fit from my data estimates each product's scale against the consensus of the others (Beyer is the fixed anchor).
